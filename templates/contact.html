About Us

It all started between lectures at Sapienza University of Rome. As two students bound by a lifelong passion for engines, sleek design, and the freedom of the open road, we constantly found ourselves dreaming of opening our own dealership. We didn't just want to hand over keys; we wanted to share the thrill of driving.

However, we realized that the future of mobility goes beyond the walls of a traditional physical showroom. Whether it is the roar of a supercar, the daily reliability of a city car, the adventurous spirit of a camper, or the eco-friendly breeze of a bike, people need flexibility. That realization became the spark for OmniDrive.

We poured our late-night study sessions and automotive obsession into building a digital-first platform that makes finding your perfect ride seamless, transparent, and exciting. What began as a conversation over a campus coffee has grown into a comprehensive mobility hub designed for everyone who loves the journey as much as the destination.

Welcome to our dream, driven by yours.

— Lorenzo Castelli & Alessio Avasilcai, Founders of OmniDrive
