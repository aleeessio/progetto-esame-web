Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/04/2026

3. Data Protection: We implement standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

If you have any questions about how we handle your data, please reach out to us via our Contact page.
